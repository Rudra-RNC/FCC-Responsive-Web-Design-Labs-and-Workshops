Aand Bhaat

The delicacy created through the combination of a staple food item with an item best known to be left untouched in the process of cooking.
A Contardictory Connoisseur's Cookbook Secret

Below is a straightforward, no-nonsense recipe for Aand Bhaat—a rustic, protein-forward egg-and-rice preparation widely consumed in parts of Chhattisgarh and eastern India. It’s designed for efficiency, affordability, and flavor density.

Ingredients

  • Eggs – 4
  • Rice – 1 cup (short or medium grain preferred)
  • Onion – 1 medium, finely chopped
  • Tomato – 1 medium, finely chopped
  • Green chilli – 1 (optional, slit)
  • Garlic – 4–5 cloves, crushed
  • Ginger – 1 tsp, grated
  • Turmeric powder – ½ tsp
  • Red chilli powder – ½ tsp
  • Coriander powder – 1 tsp
  • Cumin seeds – ½ tsp
  • Mustard oil or regular cooking oil – 2 tbsp
  • Salt – to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves – for garnish
  • Water – ~2½ cups

Instructions

  1. Prep the Rice
    Wash and soak rice for 15 minutes. Drain.
  2. Build the Base
     Heat oil in a deep pan or pressure cooker.
    Add cumin seeds; let them crackle.
    Add onion, garlic, and ginger. Sauté until golden and aromatic.
  3. Layer the Masala
     Add green chilli and tomatoes.
    Cook until oil separates.
    Add turmeric, red chilli powder, coriander powder, and salt. Stir well.
  4. Add Eggs (Key Step)
     Lower the flame. Crack eggs directly into the pan.
    Stir continuously to scramble them into the masala until cooked but moist.
  5. Incorporate Rice
     Add soaked rice. Mix thoroughly so grains are well coated.
  6. Cook
     Add water. Bring to a boil.
    Cover and cook on low flame until rice is done and water absorbed.
    (Pressure cooker: 1 whistle on low flame.)
  7. Finish
     Garnish with fresh coriander. Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.
